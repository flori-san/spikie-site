Delete your Plushie account

You can delete your account in the Plushie app (by Stonefield sp. z o.o.) or, if you no longer have the app, by email. Below is what we delete, and what we have to keep and for how long.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Plushie and swipe to the Settings page.
  2. At the bottom, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete my account. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: without the app, by email

  1. Email hello@stonefield.app with the subject "Delete my Plushie account", from the email address you use in Plushie. If you sign in with Apple and hide your email, write from the address that receives Apple's forwarded emails and say so in your message.
  2. If we can't confirm that the account is yours, we'll reply and ask you to confirm. We never ask for passwords or card details.
  3. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

When an account can be deleted. When no week is running, every charge is paid and no dispute is open. If something is still in progress, we'll tell you what we're waiting for. Usually it's enough to finish the week and its check-in. A locked week can't be stopped early, because its goals are a commitment (Terms, § 4).

What we delete straight away

  • your account and sign-in data (email address, link to Apple or Google);
  • the text of all your goals;
  • saved payment methods, also at the payment provider;
  • the text of your disputes;
  • push notification tokens for your phones;
  • statistics linked to your account.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Charge records (amount, date, status, the payment provider's transaction ID, the split between the charity and us): 5 years from the end of the year of the payment, because accounting and tax law require it. They contain no email address, name or goal text.
  • Database backups: deleted data is gone from them within 7 days.
  • Emails with us, including your deletion request: up to 3 years, in case of claims.

Plushie the cactus's progress is stored on your phone and disappears when you remove the app. More in the Privacy Policy.

Want to delete only some data?

Email hello@stonefield.app and say what you'd like deleted (for example the text of a particular goal, or a saved payment method). We'll do it within 7 days, unless the data is needed to settle a running week.
