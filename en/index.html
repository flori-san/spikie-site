Weekly goals. For real.

Pick your goals for the next 7 days and put your own money on each one. Keep your word and you pay nothing. Miss a goal and you pay, and we give half of it to a charity you choose.

How it works

  1. Write your goals. Up to 20 a week, in your own words, each with a yes-or-no answer. For example "gym 3 times" or "no phone after 10 pm".
  2. Set the stakes. 10 to 1,000 zł per goal. Nothing is charged up front.
  3. Lock the week. From then on, goals can't be changed or cancelled.
  4. Check in honestly. After 7 days you have 24 hours to mark each goal "done" or "missed".
  5. Done: you pay nothing. Missed: we charge that goal's stake. Half goes to the charity you picked, the other half keeps Plushie running.

You can't win anything in Plushie. You either keep your money or pay for a goal you missed. The app is free, and a charge only happens when you yourself report a goal as missed (or don't check in). Adults only.
