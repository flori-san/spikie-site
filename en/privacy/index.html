Privacy Policy

Effective from: 25 November 2026. Version 1.0. This is a translation; the Polish version prevails.

This policy explains what data we collect when you use the Plushie app and this website, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are. If anything is unclear, write to us at hello@stonefield.app.

In short

  • We only collect what the app needs: your email address, your goals and stakes, your weekly results, payment details and a token for notifications.
  • We never see your card number. The payment provider handles it.
  • We don't sell data, show ads or track you across other apps.
  • The charity you pick gets none of your data from us.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or through this website.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Stonefield sp. z o.o., registered in Poznań, Poland, ul. Śniadeckich 10C/33, 60-774 Poznań, KRS 0001277274, NIP 7792614016 ("we"). We run the Plushie app and this website.

For anything about your personal data, write to hello@stonefield.app or to our registered address. We have not appointed a data protection officer, because at our scale we are not required to.

4. The text of your goals

You write your goals in your own words. They may be about your health or other personal matters (e.g. "no alcohol this week"). We don't analyse the text of your goals or use it for anything except running the app. Only two people on our team can see it, and only when needed to decide a dispute or answer your request. Please don't put other people's data, or anything you don't want to share with us, in your goals.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our providers (Expo, Apple, Google, Stripe, Supabase, Cloudflare) are based in the US or may access data from the US. We only transfer data with the safeguards the GDPR requires: a European Commission adequacy decision (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) where the recipient is certified under it, or the Commission's standard contractual clauses. Write to us for a copy of these safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • While you have an account: account data, goals, weeks, payment methods, disputes and push tokens.
  • Deleted as soon as you delete your account: the account and sign-in data, your email address, the text of your goals, saved payment methods (also at the payment provider), dispute texts, push tokens and statistics linked to your account.
  • Kept after you delete your account: charge records (amount, date, status, the payment provider's transaction ID, the split between the charity and us), with no email address, name or goal text. We keep them for 5 years from the end of the year of the payment, because accounting and tax law require it.
  • Database backups are overwritten within 7 days, so deleted data is gone from them after that at the latest.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 7 days.
  • Emails with us are kept for up to 3 years after the matter is closed, in case of claims.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data and information on how we use it (access);
  • have incorrect data corrected (rectification);
  • have your data deleted, with the exceptions in section 7 (erasure);
  • restrict processing;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format (portability);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (objection);
  • complain to the Polish data protection authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl, or to the authority where you live.

To use these rights, write to hello@stonefield.app from the email address on your account. We reply within one month. Giving us data is voluntary, but without an email address and a payment method you can't use the app.

9. Automatic charges

A charge for a missed goal is made automatically, but only based on your own check-in answer, or the lack of one within 24 hours. We don't judge you, profile you or decide whether you met a goal. If you think a charge is wrong, you can open a dispute within 7 days, and a person on our team decides it (see the Terms).

10. Security

Connections to the app and this website are encrypted (HTTPS). Each user can only access their own data, and the database itself enforces this. Card data is handled only by the payment provider. Only two people on our team can use the admin panel.

11. This website and cookies

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other services (not even fonts). Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. That is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

12. Age

Plushie is only for people aged 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.
