Disagree with a particular charge? The fastest way is to open a dispute in the app, on the week's result screen (within 7 days). We handle complaints within 14 days (Terms, § 9).

Common questions

How does a week work?

You write up to 20 goals, set a stake of 10 to 1,000 zł on each, and pick a charity. Once you lock the week, the goals can't be changed. The week ends at 23:59 on the seventh day. Then you have 24 hours to mark each goal "done" or "missed".

When do I pay, and how much?

Only for a goal you missed or didn't check in on within 24 hours, and exactly its stake. We charge your saved payment method no earlier than 12 hours after the week is settled. Goals you met cost nothing, and the app itself is free.

Where does the money go?

We give half of every charge, as our own donation, to the charity you picked for that week, by bank transfer once a month. The other half keeps Plushie running. Because it's our donation, you don't get a receipt or tax relief from the charity.

I answered wrongly, or couldn't check in. What now?

On the week's result screen, tap "Something wrong? Open a dispute" and pick the goal, within 7 days. The charge is put on hold and we reply within 48 hours. If we accept the dispute, we cancel or refund the charge.

A payment failed and I can't start a new week.

After a failed payment we try 3 more times over about 5 days. If those fail too, new weeks are blocked until the charge is paid. Change your payment method in Settings and we'll retry straight away and unblock your account.

How do I change my payment method?

Settings → Payment method → Change. Your card number is handled by the payment provider; we never see it.

I'm not getting the sign-in code.

Check your spam folder and the "Promotions" or "Social" tabs. You can send a new code after 60 seconds. If you created your account with Apple or Google, sign in the same way.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that Plushie is allowed to send notifications in your phone's settings. You can change the reminder language in the app's Settings.

Can I stop a week or change a goal?

No. That's what makes it a commitment: once a week is locked, goals can't be changed, cancelled or added. If something serious and unforeseeable happened, open a dispute after the check-in.

Is this gambling?

No. You can't win anything in Plushie, and the result depends only on you. You either keep your money or pay for a goal you missed. Only stake amounts you can afford to lose.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Without the app: see Delete your account.
