§ 2. Technical requirements

  1. To use the App you need a phone with iOS 16.0 or later or Android 8.0 (API 26) or later, internet access, an email address (or an Apple or Google account) and a payment method the App supports.
  2. Reminders work if you allow the App to send notifications.
  3. You must not provide unlawful content.

§ 3. Your account and the contract

  1. Only people aged 18 or older with full legal capacity may use the App. When creating an account you confirm that you meet these conditions.
  2. The contract for using the App is concluded when you create your account, for an indefinite period. Using the App is free. The only payments are the Charges described in § 6.
  3. Each person may have one account. Your account is personal and can't be transferred to anyone else.

§ 4. Weeks, goals and stakes

  1. Each Week can have 1 to 20 Goals. Each Goal has a Stake from 10 zł to 1,000 zł. When a Week's Stakes add up to more than 1,000 zł, the App asks you to confirm once more.
  2. You pick one Charity for the whole Week.
  3. Before locking your first Week you must save a payment method. Saving it charges nothing.
  4. A Week starts when you lock it and ends at 23:59 on the seventh day, in your phone's time zone at the time of locking. You can have one running Week at a time.
  5. By locking a Week you commit to checking in honestly and accept the Charges for missed or unanswered Goals. After locking, Goals, Stakes and the Charity can't be changed, cancelled or added to.

§ 5. Checking in

  1. Check-in opens when the Week ends and lasts 24 hours. For each Goal you answer whether you did it. We remind you with notifications: when the Week ends, and after 12 and 22 hours.
  2. Each answer is final once sent.
  3. A Goal you don't answer within 24 hours counts as missed.
  4. We don't check whether Goals were met and we don't judge what they say. The result depends only on what you do and your honest answer.

§ 6. Charges for missed goals

  1. For each missed or unanswered Goal you owe us a Charge equal to its Stake. For a Goal you met, you pay nothing.
  2. Charges for all Goals from the same Week are collected together as one payment.
  3. We charge your saved payment method through our payment provider (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.), no earlier than 12 hours after the Week is settled. You agree to this by saving a payment method and locking a Week.
  4. Amounts are in Polish złoty and include VAT where it applies.
  5. If a charge fails, we retry it up to 3 times, about 1, 3 and 5 days later. If that fails too, you can't lock new Weeks until the Charge is paid. When you save a new payment method, we retry straight away.
  6. An unpaid Charge remains a debt you owe us, and we may take steps to recover it.
  7. On request, we issue an invoice or another document confirming a Charge.

§ 7. Charities

  1. We give half of every paid Charge, as our own donation, to the Charity you picked for that Week. If the amount has an odd number of grosze, the extra grosz goes to the Charity. The other half is our income.
  2. We pay these amounts once a month, by bank transfer, for the previous month. A refunded Charge is left out of the transfer.
  3. You are not making a donation. You get no donation receipt from the Charity and can't deduct the Charge from your income as a donation.
  4. The list of Charities is in the App. It never includes political parties. If a Charity stops meeting our criteria, we may remove it from the list and give its share to another Charity with a similar purpose.

§ 8. Disputing a charge

  1. If you think a Charge is wrong (for example you answered by mistake, or couldn't check in for reasons beyond your control), you can open a dispute in the App, on the Week's result screen, within 7 days of the settlement. Each Goal can have one dispute.
  2. Opening a dispute puts the Charge on hold if it hasn't been taken yet.
  3. A person on our team decides the dispute within 48 hours. You can add an explanation.
  4. If we accept the dispute, we cancel the Charge, or refund it to the same payment method if it was already taken. If we reject it, the Charge goes ahead and we explain why.
  5. A dispute doesn't limit your right to complain (§ 9) or to go to court.

§ 9. Complaints

  1. You can complain about the App or a Charge by email to hello@stonefield.app or in writing to our registered address.
  2. Say what the complaint is about and what you expect, and give the email address on your account.
  3. We reply within 14 days, to the email address the complaint came from.

§ 10. Right of withdrawal

  1. As a consumer, you can withdraw from the contract without giving a reason within 14 days of concluding it (creating your account). Just email a statement to hello@stonefield.app, for example: "I withdraw from the contract for using the Spikie app. Account email: …".
  2. If a Week is running when you withdraw, we end it with no Charges. Charges for Weeks settled before the withdrawal remain due. After the withdrawal we delete your account as described in the Privacy Policy.

§ 11. Deleting your account and ending the contract

  1. You can delete your account at any time in the App (Settings → Delete account) or through our website. Deleting your account ends the contract.
